About the practice

Noor Valen designs digital products for teams that care how things feel. The work runs from the first sketch to the shipped system, with prototypes in between that settle arguments early.

Before going independent in 2019, Noor led design at two product companies and spent a year building interfaces in code. That mix shows up in the handoff. Files are tidy, states are covered and motion arrives with timing values.

Most engagements last three to nine months and sit inside the client team. One designer, one point of contact, no layers.
