About the practice

Oculifelis designs games, mods and websites as complete systems. Every project starts as a plan, with the rules, the numbers and the look settled before anything gets built.

The work is planning and design. A project gets its scope, its mechanics, its balance tables and its art direction on paper first, and production follows that plan to the finished release.

Most released projects are Minecraft mods, and each one keeps its tables in data files that pack makers can change. Games, tools and website designs sit beside them and get the same treatment.
